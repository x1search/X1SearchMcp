Known limitations

read before quoting
  • Coefficients rest on 1–2 paired samples per category and are vendor-derived, not measured on your data. The bands widen to reflect that; they do not remove it.
  • The multiple is applied proportionally, but part of the connector's overhead (tracking URLs, duplicated identifiers) is fixed per item — so short items are under-credited and long ones over-credited.
  • Structured/tabular content claims nothing at all: no paired xlsx/csv measurement has been taken.
  • The connector's real chain for an email body is search + read_resource (two calls); this compares one call against one, under-counting its cost.
  • Cloud tables (OneDrive/GDrive/SP365) expose opaque item ids, so tabular and scanned cloud content cannot be classified by extension and is priced as text — another under-count.
  • Token counts are characters ÷ 4, not a real tokenizer. The error largely cancels in a ratio but not in absolute figures.
Every one of these leans toward claiming less. The honest way to remove them is to run the paired measurements on your own mail and files rather than inherit these coefficients.
